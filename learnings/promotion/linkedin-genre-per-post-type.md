# One LinkedIn genre does not fit every post

Benchmarked a same-niche creator's post (Durgesh Tiwari, ~9.7k followers): single-image
infographic + emoji-list body, 100 reactions ≈ 1% rate — lower than our PDF carousel's
2.3% per-impression. The format wasn't the differentiator; genre breadth was.

What the winning pattern does that ours didn't:

- **Question inside the post body** ("Are you using X in your projects?") — can't be
  skipped the way a pinned comment can.
- **Emoji-list body** (🚀 hook, ✅ benefits) — scannable in two seconds.
- **Opinionated closer** — a take, not just a CTA.
- **~8 targeted hashtags** including a personal brand tag.
- **Broad-accessible topic** — boilerplate everyone recognizes beats a deep-niche
  tuning post for reach.

Rule: keep PDF carousels for benchmark/reference posts (saves + dwell are their
metrics); for broad topics use a single-image infographic + emoji-list body — cheap to
produce (one hero slide from generate_carousel.py) and native-feeling. Always put the
discussion question in the body; the pinned comment is a backup, not the mechanism.
